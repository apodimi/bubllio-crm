# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Bubllio is for people who manage customer relationships throughout their working day. Their
primary job is to find the right customer or contact quickly, including while working from a
phone.

## Product Purpose

Bubllio keeps companies, contacts, follow-ups, services, and billing in one tenant-safe workspace.
Success means reaching the relevant customer record and next action
with minimal navigation or data entry.

## Positioning

A focused, self-hostable CRM that connects relationship context with operational follow-ups and
customer services without the complexity of a broad enterprise suite.

## Operating Context

Users work across desktop and mobile. Frequent workflows include finding a company or contact,
checking relationship context, completing a follow-up, and reviewing services or billing that
need attention.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Multi-tenant workspaces with strict organization boundaries.
- Companies, contacts, tasks, deals, customer services, and billing.
- Mobile access is a first-class requirement, especially customer and contact discovery.
- Django REST Framework remains authoritative for validation and authorization.
- The React client uses Material UI and the existing application architecture.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the existing Bubllio name, logo, and brand colors.
- The interface should feel lighter and less visually bulky without weakening usability.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the working product, real domain terminology, the Bubllio mark, established
brand tokens, and browser-tested workflows. No customer testimonials or external performance claims
are available and none should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Make customer and contact discovery immediate.
- Design frequent actions for one-handed mobile use as well as desktop efficiency.
- Show operational information only when it helps the user decide or act.
- Prefer direct workflows and plain language over configuration-heavy interaction.
- Preserve tenant safety and server-authoritative business rules.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, touch-friendly targets, and responsive
layouts are required across core workflows.
