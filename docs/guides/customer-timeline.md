# Customer Timeline

Open a company and scroll to **Customer timeline** to see its relationship and
operational history in one chronological list.

## What appears

The timeline records:

- company and contact changes;
- deals and stage changes;
- task creation, updates, completion, and reopening;
- customer service creation, updates, and status changes;
- recorded payments;
- manual notes added by workspace members who can manage CRM records.

Use the category controls to focus on notes, tasks, deals, contacts, services,
or payments. Choose **Load earlier activity** when more than 25 entries match.

## Add customer context

Use **Add a note** for information that does not belong in a structured field,
such as a decision, conversation outcome, or agreed next step. Notes are visible
to every member who can view that workspace. The first version does not edit or
delete timeline notes.

Do not place passwords, credentials, payment-card data, authentication tokens,
or other secrets in a note.

## Existing records

The timeline migration preserves existing company/contact activity and creates
historical entries for existing deals, tasks, customer services, and payments.
Older records can therefore appear with **System** as the actor when the original
operation did not store one.

Timeline events are history. Deleting or archiving a related record does not
rewrite earlier entries, although deleting the company removes its timeline with
the rest of that company's data.
